Accounts

Account types that make sense for a bank portal.

Accounts are the foundation. Savings handles everyday banking, fixed deposit account handles locked-term deposits, and investment account gives you a credible future product area without overloading the MVP.

Everyday Savings

The customer's main account for deposits, balance checks, transfers, linked cards, and transaction history.

  • • Available and ledger balance
  • • Incoming and outgoing transfers
  • • Card linkage and account statements

Fixed Deposit Account

A dedicated account product for placing funds over a chosen tenure, tracking maturity date, and paying returns to a linked account.

  • • Placement amount and tenure
  • • Projected interest calculation
  • • Maturity and payout instruction

Investment Account

A future-ready product area for risk profile, investment eligibility, holdings summary, and portfolio-style reporting.

  • • Risk profile assessment
  • • Holdings summary concept
  • • Product suitability workflow

Cards

Card products with different customer use cases.

Cards are not features; they are product lines. Each one can share the same card table underneath while using different limits, eligibility, and dashboard labels.

Debit card

Everyday Debit

Linked directly to the savings account for daily spending, ATM withdrawals, and card activity tracking.

Student card

Campus Card

A student-friendly card concept with lower limits, clear controls, and simple account linkage.

Money Transfers

Move funds with review, verification, and clean records.

A strong transfer flow should show source account, recipient, amount, remarks, confirmation, OTP if needed, and a final transaction receipt.

Before sending

Display account, recipient, amount, and remarks clearly.

After sending

Save the record into transfers and transaction history.

Fixed Deposits

A deposit flow that feels real, not just a form.

Customers choose a source account, placement amount, tenure, maturity instruction, and see projected returns before confirming. Admin or system logic can later process maturity and payout.

Loans

Loan management from application to repayment.

A realistic loan module should not only take an amount. It should capture the purpose, requested tenure, application status, approval decision, disbursement account, and repayment summary.

Application Review

Store loan type, amount, applicant details, and decision result.

Disbursement

Approved loan amount can be deposited into the selected account.

Repayment Summary

Show estimated instalments, interest, and remaining balance.

Loan History

Show active, approved, rejected, and completed applications.
