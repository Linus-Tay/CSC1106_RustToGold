Controls

A practical security model for your banking app.

Login protection

Use hashed passwords, secure sessions, expiry, and role-based redirects for customer and admin users.

OTP verification

Use OTP for sensitive actions such as high-value transfers, profile changes, or new payee setup.

Activity alerts

Notify customers when cards are frozen, transfers are completed, or account status changes.

Suspicious activity review

Repeated high-value actions can trigger review, temporary restrictions, or extra verification.

Card freeze controls

A simple freeze and unfreeze flow helps customers respond quickly when a card is misplaced.

Audit logging

Log admin approvals, loan decisions, account status changes, and other sensitive updates.
