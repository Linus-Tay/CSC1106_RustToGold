Common questions

Everything important, explained clearly.

These answers cover the most common things customers may want to know before opening an account, applying for a product, or managing their banking online.

What can I do with a RustToGold account?

You can view account balances, manage everyday banking activity, make transfers, review card access, explore fixed deposits, and keep track of product applications from one digital banking experience.

How do I open an account?

Start by choosing the account that fits your needs, then complete the online application with your personal details. Once submitted, you can follow the application status and next steps clearly.

Can I apply for cards through RustToGold?

Yes. You can apply for supported card products, view card status, and manage card-related actions such as freezing a card when you need to protect it quickly.

When is OTP verification used?

OTP verification may be required for higher-risk actions such as large transfers, new payee setup, sensitive profile changes, or actions that need extra confirmation before they are completed.

What should I do if my card is lost or something looks suspicious?

Freeze your card as soon as possible from your banking dashboard. This helps reduce further card activity while you review the issue or contact support.

How do activity alerts help?

Activity alerts keep you updated when important actions happen, such as transfers, login activity, account changes, card status updates, or security checks.

Can I place a fixed deposit online?

Yes. You can review fixed deposit options, choose a placement amount and tenure, and view maturity details before confirming your placement.

How are loan applications managed?

Loan applications can be submitted and tracked online. You can review your application details, status updates, approval outcome, and repayment information when available.

Is my account activity recorded?

Important account actions are recorded to help keep your banking history clear and traceable. This supports better support handling, account review, and security checks.

How do I contact RustToGold support?

Use the contact form to send your enquiry. For security reasons, never share your password, full card number, or OTP in a support message.
