About RustToGold

Digital banking designed to feel clear, secure, and practical.

RustToGold brings account opening, online banking, admin review, security controls, database-backed records, and role-based access into one banking experience.

Built for

Realistic workflows

Focus

Secure banking UI

Users

Customer + Admin

Approach

Clean MVC structure

Design principles

Clear enough for users, structured enough for developers.

Believable banking

The pages avoid fake enterprise fluff and focus on services people expect from a bank: accounts, cards, transfers, deposits, loans, dashboard, and support.

Modular structure

Navigation, footer, layout, colours, cards, buttons, and page sections are reusable so the project stays maintainable.

Trust by design

Security and deposit insurance content are placed where customers can see them, not hidden at the bottom of the experience.

What it provides

A complete banking workflow from onboarding to account management.

Customer experience

Landing, account opening, login, dashboard, transfers, cards, deposits, and support.

Admin workflow

Review applications, approve loans, monitor suspicious actions, and maintain audit trails.

Security thinking

OTP checks, session protection, card freeze, role-based access, and traceable changes.

Banking data model

Users, profiles, accounts, transactions, transfers, cards, loans, fixed deposits, and logs.
